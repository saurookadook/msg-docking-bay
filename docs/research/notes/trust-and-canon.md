# Trust, Canon, and Authoritative References for Gundam Mobile Suit / Series Data

Research scope note: about 20 tool calls. Several pages could not be fetched: Mecha Talk returned 403, and some gundam.fandom.com policy pages returned 402 (rate-limited) after a few successful fetches. gundam-official.com's /series index rendered empty because it is a client-side app. Where a claim could not be verified from a fetched page, it is placed under Gaps.

## 1. What are the authoritative primary references for Gundam mecha specs and timelines, and how do fan sites use them?

### Takeaway
Sunrise (now Bandai Namco Filmworks) treats animated works as the top source. After them come Tomino's novels, then Sunrise-supervised publications (reference books, file collections), then other approved material such as model kit manuals and games. The best-run fan wiki (Gundam Wiki on Fandom) mirrors this order in its Reliable Sources policy and cites model kit manuals by JAN number. MAHQ and Wikipedia give specs with little or no per-field citation.

### Cited Findings
- Sunrise's position, as compiled by fans from Sunrise statements (citing GM vol. 15, 2005): "Sunrise-approved books, games, and models are all official." Film takes priority but does not cancel other sources. The listed priority is: (1) animated works, (2) creator Tomino's novels, (3) Sunrise-supervised publications, (4) approved supplementary materials. Animated sources override lower-priority material where they contradict. — [Pastebin compilation "Sunrise's stance on Gundam canon"](https://pastebin.com/LfFAeqSW) (secondary fan compilation, not a primary Sunrise document; the same topic is discussed at [Mecha Talk "Sunrise's stance on canon"](https://mechatalk.net/viewtopic.php?t=16346), which returned 403 when fetched)
- Gundam Wiki Reliable Sources policy: "The most reliable sources are the official works of Gundam fiction that are created by or under license from Sunrise." Episodes and chapters are cited with Template:ref. Accepted supplementary sources are printed media (Template:ref book), model kit and figure instruction manuals (Template:Cite book with release date and JAN number, linking unwatermarked scans where possible), file collections and official file sheets, physical AV media (DVD/Blu-ray), and web sources. Policy line: "If no reliable sources can be found on a topic, Gundam Wiki should not have an article on it." — [Gundam Wiki:Reliable Sources](https://gundam.fandom.com/wiki/Gundam_Wiki:Reliable_Sources)
- The Reliable Sources policy does not list MAHQ, Wikipedia, Japanese wikis or video games as acceptable sources. — [Gundam Wiki:Reliable Sources](https://gundam.fandom.com/wiki/Gundam_Wiki:Reliable_Sources)
- The Gundam Wiki keeps a dedicated article on *Mobile Suit Gundam Official Encyclopedia GUNDAM OFFICIALS*, which shows it is treated as a citable reference work. — [Gundam Wiki: Gundam Officials](https://gundam.fandom.com/wiki/Gundam_Officials) (page returned 402 on fetch; title confirmed from search results only)
- The Gundam Wiki RX-78-2 article cites "Gundam Officials," "Gundam Century," and Bandai model manuals. — [Gundam Wiki: RX-78-2 Gundam](https://gundam.fandom.com/wiki/RX-78-2_Gundam)
- MAHQ's RX-78-2 page gives a full spec block with no source citations. It credits designers (Kunio Okawara; Hajime Katoki and Mika Akitaka for alternate versions) and shows a date of February 10, 2022. — [MAHQ: RX-78-2 Gundam](https://www.mahq.net/rx-78-2/)
- Wikipedia's article on the fictional robot gives only height (18 m) and weight (43.4 t). It cites a single archived Bandai USA "Mechanics: Gundam" page (retrieved 2008) for mechanical data. — [Wikipedia: Gundam (fictional robot)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gundam_(fictional_robot))
- Official publishing continues. For example, an official *GQuuuuuuX Animation Guide* was announced on the official site. — [Gundam Official news: GQuuuuuuX Animation Guide](https://en.gundam-official.com/news/so7yj69zbj9d9p26j3mo3anw)

### Inferences
- Trust order for a data app: (1) the official works and the official site (gundam-official.com) for titles, dates and timeline placement; (2) Sunrise-supervised reference books (Gundam Officials, Perfect Files, Master Archive, Mobile Suit Bible, Gundam Chronicle and similar) for specs; (3) model kit manuals, which are official but often add or alter specs per kit (e.g., Master Grade "Ver. Ka" bios); (4) fan aggregators, ranked by how well they cite (1)–(3).
- Of the fan sources, the Gundam Wiki is the only one found with a written sourcing policy tied to official materials. That makes it the most auditable aggregator. MAHQ is internally consistent and curated but unauditable because it has no citations. Wikipedia is thin on specs.

### Gaps
- Bibliographic details (publisher, year, scope) for Gundam Officials, Gundam Perfect Files, the Mobile Suit Bible weekly partwork, Master Archive, Gundam Chronicle and the Gundam Mecha Encyclopedia could not be verified: the Gundam Officials page returned 402 and the others were not reached. Background understanding, unverified this session: Gundam Officials is a Kodansha-published UC encyclopedia (c. 2001); Master Archive is a SB Creative series; the Mobile Suit Bible is a Deagostini weekly. The report writer should not state these as fact without verification.
- MAHQ's sourcing methodology is not documented. Its About page lists contributors (Six Dettmar, Pedro Cortes, Chris Guanche) and "©2000" by Accidental Pilot, Inc., but no source policy. — [MAHQ About](https://www.mahq.net/about.htm)

## 2. How is canon handled in Gundam? Conflicting specs, retcons (The Origin, UC NexT 0100), and who tracks discrepancies

### Takeaway
Gundam has no binary "canon." Sunrise separates "official" (公式, sanctioned setting information) from "canon" (正史, "correct history"), and ranks information by medium, with animation first. Conflicting specs across books, kits, games and remakes such as The Origin are therefore normal and expected. None of the major fan sources reviewed systematically tracks per-field spec discrepancies.

### Cited Findings
- Shigeru Horiguchi of Sunrise said in 2001: "If it's filmed, it's official." This was widely misread as rejecting all non-film works. Later clarification made a distinction: "Official" is Sunrise-sanctioned material whose setting information counts as part of the universe; "Canon" is what actually happened in the story. Setting details from non-film sources such as the *Gihren's Greed* games can be official even though their stories are not canon. — [Pastebin compilation](https://pastebin.com/LfFAeqSW)
- The Gundam Wiki Canon Policy is an editorial taxonomy, not a canon declaration. Animated media take precedence: "When an adaptation is animated and its source material is not, then the adaptation becomes the main version." Examples: the 2021 *Hathaway* film supersedes the 1989 novel; the 1979 TV series takes precedence over the 2001 manga (The Origin). An adaptation gets separate articles only if it is "different enough to its source material." Compilation films do not get separate articles. — [Gundam Wiki:Canon Policy](https://gundam.fandom.com/wiki/Gundam_Wiki:Canon_Policy)
- Example of a distinct-version entity: the Gundam Wiki keeps separate pages for "RX-78-02 Gundam" (The Origin version) and "RX-78F00 Gundam" (the Gundam Factory Yokohama 1:1 version), alongside RX-78-2. — [Gundam Wiki: RX-78-02 Gundam](https://gundam.fandom.com/wiki/RX-78-02_Gundam); [Gundam Wiki: RX-78F00 Gundam](https://gundam.fandom.com/wiki/RX-78F00_Gundam)
- MAHQ instead lists the model number as "RX-78-2/RX-78-02", merging The Origin version into one entry. Its specs: head height 18.0 m; empty weight 43.4 t; max gross 60.0 t; 1,380 kW; total thrust given as "55,599 kg" by the fetch tool (possibly a transcription of 55,500; verify on page); 5,700 m sensor range. — [MAHQ: RX-78-2](https://www.mahq.net/rx-78-2/)
- The Gundam Wiki RX-78-2 Notes & Trivia section records narrative discrepancies between media; for example, the novel has the Gundam destroyed differently from the TV series. Per the fetch, it does not systematically compare weight, thrust or sensor specs across sources. — [Gundam Wiki: RX-78-2 Gundam](https://gundam.fandom.com/wiki/RX-78-2_Gundam)
- Wikipedia does not discuss spec discrepancies between versions (The Origin, GQuuuuuuX, etc.) in its RX-78-2 article. — [Wikipedia: Gundam (fictional robot)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gundam_(fictional_robot))
- UC NexT 0100 is "a project announced by Sunrise in 2018" to "expand on the period between the events of Unicorn and F91, as well as the era after Victory." The Gundam Wiki lists Char's Counterattack (UC0093), Unicorn (0096), Narrative (0097), Hathaway (0105), F91 (0123) and Victory (0153) as anchor points. — [Gundam Wiki: UC NexT 0100](https://gundam.fandom.com/wiki/UC_NexT_0100)
- GQuuuuuuX is set in "an alternate version of the Universal Century timeline" in which Char seizes the Gundam and Zeon wins the One Year War. The TV story takes place in UC 0085. It is co-produced by Studio Khara and Sunrise, "the first Gundam production to be co-produced by Studio Khara." — [Wikipedia: Mobile Suit Gundam GQuuuuuuX](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mobile_Suit_Gundam_GQuuuuuuX)
- Third-party explainers on the "Gundam timeline tangle" exist; not fetched. — [All The Anime blog](https://blog.alltheanime.com/the-gundam-timeline-tangle/); [MyAnimeList: Timelines of Gundam guide](https://myanimelist.net/featured/1603/The_Timelines_of_Gundam__Ultimate_Beginners_Guide)

### Inferences
- Data model implication: the app should model **variants/versions** (e.g., RX-78-2 vs RX-78-02 Origin vs alternate-UC GQuuuuuuX versions) and **source-attributed spec values** (value plus source reference), not one "true" value per field. Sources that separate versions (the Gundam Wiki) map more cleanly onto that model than those that merge them (MAHQ).
- Alternate-continuity works inside a timeline (GQuuuuuuX's alternate UC) mean "universe/timeline" alone is not enough as a key. A "continuity" or "work" dimension is needed.
- None of the sources reviewed keeps per-field spec conflict tables. The Gundam Wiki comes closest through its version split and its citation of specific manuals and books.

### Gaps
- No primary Sunrise or Bandai Namco Filmworks document on canon was retrieved. The 2001 Horiguchi quote and the 2005 GM vol. 15 clarification are known only through a fan compilation (Pastebin) and a Mecha Talk thread that could not be fetched.
- Concrete examples of conflicting RX-78-2 numbers across books (e.g., different weights or outputs in Origin-era materials versus 1979-era materials) were not found in fetched sources. Do not assert specific conflicting figures without verification.
- How UC NexT 0100 or The Origin formally retconned earlier specs or events was not documented in fetched sources.

## 3. Is there an official list of Gundam universes/timelines and series, and how current is it in 2026?

### Takeaway
The official English site (en.gundam-official.com) has a per-work "series" page structure (e.g., /series/gquuuuuux-beginning, /series/hathaway-i) that covers current releases. It works as the official work list, but the index is a JavaScript app and was not crawlable by a simple fetch. The Gundam Wiki keeps a curated ten-timeline list. 2025–2026 brought GQuuuuuuX (2025), the second Hathaway film (Jan 2026), and announcements such as a new "long" Gundam Wing animation.

### Cited Findings
- The official site has per-work pages, e.g., "Mobile Suit Gundam GQuuuuuuX -Beginning-" and "Mobile Suit Gundam Hathaway". — [gundam-official: GQuuuuuuX -Beginning-](https://en.gundam-official.com/series/gquuuuuux-beginning); [gundam-official: Hathaway](https://en.gundam-official.com/series/hathaway-i)
- Fetching https://en.gundam-official.com/series returned only metadata and no content. The page is client-rendered, so a crawler needs a headless browser or the site's underlying data endpoints. — fetch result for [en.gundam-official.com/series](https://en.gundam-official.com/series)
- Gundam Wiki's timeline list: Universal Century; Future Century (G Gundam); After Colony / Mars Century (Wing); After War (X); Correct Century (∀); Cosmic Era (SEED); Anno Domini (00); Advanced Generation (AGE); Regild Century (G-Reco, about 1,000 years after UC); Post Disaster (IBO). — [Gundam Wiki:Timelines](https://gundam.fandom.com/wiki/Gundam_Wiki:Timelines)
- GQuuuuuuX: the *Beginning* compilation film opened January 17, 2025; the TV series ran April 9 – June 25, 2025 (12 episodes). — [Wikipedia: GQuuuuuuX](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mobile_Suit_Gundam_GQuuuuuuX)
- *Mobile Suit Gundam Hathaway – The Sorcery of Nymph Circe* opened theatrically in Japan in January 2026. In other 2026 news: the GQuuuuuuX Blu-ray sets (four volumes, Jan–Mar) and a 4K UHD *Beginning* set (May); the *Endless Waltz* 4K remaster (March, 30th anniversary); a G Gundam 30th-anniversary short; the Gundam X 30th-anniversary logo; a "long" Gundam Wing animation confirmed in development at Gundam Conference (May); a First Gundam remaster announced; the live-action Gundam movie cast revealed (April); the Odaiba 1:1 Unicorn statue scheduled to retire in September 2026. — [GundamPixel "This year in Gundam (so far!)", June 1, 2026](https://www.gundampixel.com/blog/2026-in-gundam-1) (fan news blog; secondary)
- Hathaway: The Sorcery of Nymph Circe has an August 2026 Netflix release date and a separate US theatrical release. — [What's on Netflix](https://www.whats-on-netflix.com/news/anime/mobile-suit-gundam-hathaway-sorcery-of-nymph-circe-netflix-release-date/); [CBR](https://www.cbr.com/gundam-hathaway-sorcery-of-nymph-circe-america-theater-release-date/); [Gizmodo (Jan 2026 Japan release)](https://gizmodo.com/gundam-hathaway-2-trailer-sorcery-of-nymph-circe-nycc-2025-2000670699)

### Inferences
- The Gundam Wiki's ten-timeline list does not have a separate entry for GQuuuuuuX's alternate UC, The Witch from Mercury's Ad Stella era, SEED Freedom, or the Build-series meta-settings, at least not in the fetched summary. A complete app taxonomy should check the official site's per-work pages for newer works rather than relying on the wiki's timeline page alone.
- For "currency," the official site is the authority for release metadata, and fan wikis lag behind it. Treat gundam-official.com as the source of truth for the work list and dates.

### Gaps
- Could not confirm whether gundam-official.com publishes an explicit grouping by timeline/universe or a machine-readable work list (its index did not render). The Japanese site (gundam-official.com, JP) was not checked.

## 4. Community reputation for accuracy: Gundam Wiki (Fandom), MAHQ, Wikipedia, Japanese wikis

### Takeaway
Based on policies and observed pages (not community polling, which could not be retrieved), the Gundam Wiki has the strongest written sourcing and canon policies of the English sources. MAHQ is a long-running (since 2000), curated, consistently formatted spec database without citations. Wikipedia covers works well (dates, staff, production) but covers mecha specs only thinly. No Reddit or forum reputation data could be retrieved.

### Cited Findings
- Gundam Wiki has formal policy pages: Canon Policy, Reliable Sources, Standards, Image Policy and an FAQ. — [Canon Policy](https://gundam.fandom.com/wiki/Gundam_Wiki:Canon_Policy); [Reliable Sources](https://gundam.fandom.com/wiki/Gundam_Wiki:Reliable_Sources); [Standards](https://gundam.fandom.com/wiki/Gundam_Wiki:Standards); [Image Policy](https://gundam.fandom.com/wiki/Gundam_Wiki:Image_Policy); [FAQ](https://gundam.fandom.com/wiki/Gundam_Wiki:Frequently_Asked_Questions)
- Gundam Wiki explicitly bars articles that lack reliable official sources ("should not have an article on it"), which guards against fanon. — [Reliable Sources](https://gundam.fandom.com/wiki/Gundam_Wiki:Reliable_Sources)
- MAHQ: "Your source for mecha anime info." Original content "©2000" by Accidental Pilot, Inc. Covers 40+ series, with an active community (podcast "Gundamn!", Discord, YouTube, Bluesky). No accuracy or sourcing statement. — [MAHQ About](https://www.mahq.net/about.htm); [Gundamn! @ MAHQ podcast](https://gundamn.podbean.com/)
- MAHQ has undergone community-driven reorganization efforts discussed on Mecha Talk ("The MAHQ Grand Burkean Reorganization Project"). — [Mecha Talk thread](https://mechatalk.net/viewtopic.php?t=14544) (not fetched; title only)
- Wikipedia RX-78-2 mecha data rests on one archived Bandai USA page from 2008. — [Wikipedia: Gundam (fictional robot)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gundam_(fictional_robot))
- Vandalism/COI precedent: Anime News Network reported in 2007 that Japanese government officials were reprimanded over Gundam-related Wikipedia edits. — [ANN, 2007-10-04](https://www.animenewsnetwork.com/news/2007-10-04/government-gundam-wikipedia) (headline from search result; article not fetched)
- Other fan sites exist but are lower-tier (fan-maintained; e.g., OBD Wiki mecha profiles; MechaBay; character-profile/VS-debate Fandom wikis that inflate or interpret specs). — [OBD Wiki RX-78-2](https://outskirtsbattledomewiki.com/index.php?view=article&id=2542&catid=16); [MechaBay RX-78-2](https://mechabay.com/rx-78-2-gundam); [Character Profile Wiki](https://characterprofile.fandom.com/wiki/RX-78-2_Gundam); [Death Battle Wiki](https://deathbattle.fandom.com/wiki/RX-78-2_Gundam)

### Inferences
- Suggested trust ranking among crawlable fan sources for mobile suit data: (1) Gundam Wiki (Fandom), for its policies, citations to official books and manuals, and separation of versions; (2) MAHQ, for curated, consistent spec blocks (good field coverage, no citations, specs merged across versions); (3) English Wikipedia, which is reliable for series and work metadata but not mecha specs. VS-debate and character-profile wikis should be excluded as non-authoritative.
- Fandom's open editing means quality varies by page. The policies show intent, not page-level guarantees.

### Gaps
- No r/Gundam or forum threads assessing the accuracy of the Gundam Wiki, MAHQ or Wikipedia were retrieved. Search returned nothing on point, and Mecha Talk returned 403. Community reputation claims beyond policy analysis are unverified.
- Japanese sources (Japanese Wikipedia, the Japanese Gundam wikis on wikiwiki/atwiki, Pixiv Encyclopedia, Bandai Hobby Site kit pages) were not researched. They are likely closer to primary Japanese reference books but were not verified.
- The Gundam Wiki Standards page (infobox and spec rules) returned 402, so its exact content is unverified.

## 5. Is the Gundam Wiki still on Fandom, or has it forked or migrated?

### Takeaway
I found no evidence of a Gundam Wiki fork or migration off Fandom. In October 2026, gundam.fandom.com is live, its policy pages are served, and its Discussions board is active. This contrasts with prominent migrations such as Minecraft and the Falcom wikis. Treat gundam.fandom.com as the active copy unless the catalog researchers find otherwise.

### Cited Findings
- gundam.fandom.com pages (policies, timelines, RX-78-2, UC NexT 0100) were fetched successfully this session, with no "this wiki has moved" banner reported in the fetched content. — [Gundam Wiki:Timelines](https://gundam.fandom.com/wiki/Gundam_Wiki:Timelines); [Gundam Wiki: The Gundam Wiki](https://gundam.fandom.com/wiki/The_Gundam_Wiki); [Discussions board](https://gundam.fandom.com/f)
- Context: many wikis have left Fandom (e.g., Minecraft Wiki; the Falcom series wikis forming an independent Falcom Wiki). There are general fork resources such as Fork Fandom, and Fandom has its own Forking Policy. — [Yahoo Tech: Minecraft Wiki completes exit from Fandom](https://tech.yahoo.com/general/articles/minecraft-wiki-completes-exit-fandom-141218014.html); [ResetEra: Falcom wikis migrating](https://www.resetera.com/threads/multiple-falcom-series-wikis-trails-ys-xanadu-gagharv-migrating-away-from-fandom-to-form-independent-falcom-wiki.1282908/); [Fork Fandom](https://forkfandom.com/); [Fandom Forking Policy](https://community.fandom.com/wiki/Forking_Policy)
- Searches for Gundam Wiki on wiki.gg, Miraheze, or a "gundam.wiki" domain returned no Gundam-specific results. — search results recorded this session (no relevant URL)

### Inferences
- The absence of evidence is moderately strong because several targeted queries found nothing. It is not conclusive, since a small fork might not be indexed.
- Fandom's 402 responses during this session suggest it rate-limits automated access. That matters for crawling: prefer the MediaWiki API (api.php) with polite rate limits. Content is CC BY-SA, so attribution is required.

### Gaps
- No definitive statement from Gundam Wiki admins on staying or moving was found. The Fork Fandom directory listing for Gundam was not checked.
- The CC BY-SA licence for Fandom content is a known general Fandom term; it was not verified on the Gundam Wiki itself this session.

## 6. Standard data model / fields for a mobile suit entry across trusted sources

### Takeaway
MAHQ's spec block is the most standardized field set and closely mirrors the format of Japanese official reference books. Model number, unit type/classification, manufacturer, operator, pilots, head height, weights, armaments and appearances are reliably present for major units. First-deployment date, power output, thrust, sensor range and performance figures are frequently missing or vary by source, especially for non-UC and recent units.

### Cited Findings
- MAHQ RX-78-2 fields: Model number; Unit type; Manufacturer; Operator; First deployment; Accommodation; Dimensions (head height); Weight (empty / max gross); Armor materials; Powerplant (type + output kW); Propulsion (total thrust in kg); Performance (turn time, max ground speed); Equipment and design features (sensor range, etc.); Fixed armaments; Optional hand armaments; Pilots; plus designer credits. Example values: 18.0 m, 43.4 t empty / 60.0 t gross, Luna Titanium, 1,380 kW, ~55,500–55,599 kg thrust (verify exact figure), 5,700 m sensors, first deployment 18 September UC 0079. — [MAHQ: RX-78-2](https://www.mahq.net/rx-78-2/)
- Gundam Wiki RX-78-2 content (as fetched) includes model number, classification, manufacturer, operator, pilot, height 18 m, Luna Titanium armor, and beam rifle (1.9 MW) and beam saber (0.38 MW) outputs, with citations to Gundam Officials, Gundam Century and model manuals. Note: the fetch tool did not parse the infobox as a structured table, so the full field list is unconfirmed. — [Gundam Wiki: RX-78-2 Gundam](https://gundam.fandom.com/wiki/RX-78-2_Gundam)
- Wikipedia gives only height (18 m) and weight (43.4 t). — [Wikipedia: Gundam (fictional robot)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gundam_(fictional_robot))
- The Gundam Wiki cites model kit manuals with JAN numbers and release dates. This gives a stable identifier for a spec's provenance. — [Gundam Wiki:Reliable Sources](https://gundam.fandom.com/wiki/Gundam_Wiki:Reliable_Sources)

### Inferences
- Recommended core schema: `model_number`, `name`, `variant_of` / `version` (e.g., Origin, alternate UC), `work_first_appearance`, `timeline/continuity`, `unit_type`, `manufacturer`, `operator`, `pilots[]`, `designers[]`, `head_height_m`, `overall_height_m`, `empty_weight_t`, `gross_weight_t`, `armor`, `generator_output_kw`, `total_thrust_kg`, `sensor_range_m`, `first_deployment`, `fixed_armaments[]`, `hand_armaments[]`, `appearances[]`. Every spec value should carry a `source` reference (book, manual JAN, episode).
- Reliability by field, from the pages compared: model number, name, pilots and height agree across MAHQ, Gundam Wiki and Wikipedia for the RX-78-2 (18 m; 43.4 t where given). The engineering specs (output, thrust, sensor range) appear only in MAHQ and the Gundam Wiki, and these are where official books are known to diverge.
- Two numbers need checking: MAHQ shows 1,380 kW, and the beam rifle output in the Gundam Wiki is 1.9 MW. These are different quantities (generator vs weapon), not a conflict. The report writer should not conflate them.

### Gaps
- Field availability for non-UC series (SEED, 00, IBO, Witch from Mercury, GQuuuuuuX) was not sampled. Recent series often publish fewer engineering specs officially, but this was not verified here.
- The Gundam Wiki's standard infobox field list (Template:Mobile Suit) could not be confirmed (Standards page returned 402).
